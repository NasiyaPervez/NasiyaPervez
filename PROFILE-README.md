## 📦 Featured projects

**Production & flow-shop scheduling**

- **flowshop-milp-gurobi** — Permutation flow-shop makespan MILP (Bowman model, time-indexed) solved with Gurobi on the VFR benchmarks. *Gurobi · MILP · Big-M*
- **flowshop-heuristics-neh-spt** — NEH best-insertion vs SPT dispatching, benchmarked on 5- and 25-job instances with Gantt charts. *Heuristics · comparison*
- **knapsack-rl-policies** — The online knapsack problem with six decision policies, from greedy and threshold rules up to a **Monte-Carlo RL** agent and a dynamic-programming offline optimum. *RL · simulation*
- **production-scheduling-gurobi** — Workforce shift scheduling (with infeasibility-recovery via relaxation, soft constraints, and Big-M) and machine-scheduling case studies. *Gurobi*

**Logistics & workforce optimization**

- **nurse-rostering-fix-and-optimize** — Nurse rostering on the Ikegami benchmark: exact Gurobi MILP versus a **Fix-and-Optimize** sliding-window heuristic. *Heuristics · Gurobi · XML data*
- **networkx-transportation-graphs** — Graph analysis for transportation networks with NetworkX. *NetworkX · graphs*

**Simulation & modelling**

- **simulation-logistics-anylogic** — Discrete-event simulation of a logistics service built in AnyLogic, with the full project protocol. *DES · AnyLogic*
- **modelling-and-solving-optimization** — A curated set of AMPL exercises (LP, blending, max-flow, knapsack, scheduling, Sudoku, MST, TSP) plus Gurobi Python tutorials. *AMPL · Gurobi*

**Data & econometrics**

- **econometrics-r-exercises** — Applied econometrics in R: regression, panel fixed effects, difference-in-differences, IV/2SLS (quarter-of-birth), and regression discontinuity. *R · causal inference*
- **data-analysis-projects** — Business data analysis in Python: food-delivery cost & profitability (with policy simulation) and Instagram engagement EDA. *pandas · matplotlib · EDA*
